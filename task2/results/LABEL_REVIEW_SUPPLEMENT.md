# Task 2 label review

All 88 selected grounding pairs across 22 sheets were visually reviewed independently of model predictions, following the manual’s instruction on page 10 to inspect labels.

Uncertain cases were recorded, including small or occluded objects, reflections, counting units, and category boundaries. These flags document uncertainty rather than confirmed label errors. Contact-sheet inspection does not guarantee annotation completeness.

Original labels, selected examples, and primary scores remain unchanged. No examples were relabeled or excluded. Per-pair observations are recorded in `label_review_supplement.json`.
